Help & Support

How do I create an invoice?
Go to Invoices page → click “New Invoice” → select a customer, add products, then click Save Invoice.
How do I convert a quotation to invoice?
Open Quotations → click “Convert” in the Actions column, or click “Convert to Invoice” on the Create Quotation form.
How do I record a payment?
Open an invoice → click “Record Payment”, or go to Payments → “+ Record Payment”.
How do I export a report?
Go to Reports page → click “Export Report” button at the top right.
How to change font size?
Click the Settings icon ( ⚙ ) in the top bar → choose your preferred font size under Appearance.
